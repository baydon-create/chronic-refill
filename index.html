Methodology
How every figure in ChronicRx is calculated

Next due date

Last dispense date + Refill cycle (days)

Refill status

Days to due below 0 → Overdue · 0 → Due today · 1–7 → Next 7 days · above 7 → On track

Cycle progress

Days since last dispense ÷ Refill cycle, capped at 100%.

Upsell per refill

Full Rx value − Last dispensed when the dispense type is Partial or Hospital copy and the full value is higher; otherwise 0.

Baseline & potential

Baseline = Last dispensed (Full value if the Rx is Full and no last value was entered). Potential = Baseline + Upsell.

Refills in window

Dates First, First + cycle, First + 2×cycle … inside the window, where First is the later of Next due and Today.

Monthly run-rate

Σ Revenue per refill × 30 ÷ Refill cycle across all patients.

Revenue at risk

Σ Baseline of every overdue patient.

Call conversion

"Patient coming" calls ÷ All calls × 100 over the last 30 days.
